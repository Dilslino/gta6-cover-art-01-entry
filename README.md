# Leonida After Dark — Cinematic Cover Studio

Turn your own photos and a caption into a GTA-style poster cover: one large hero panel, a strip of supporting photo panels, a Vice City skyline painted procedurally in code, bold layered caption lettering, halftone print texture and film grain — exported as a PNG in the post sizes you actually share (1080×1080, 1080×1350, 1920×1080). Two colour grades (Golden Hour / Neon Night), three lettering styles, per-panel crop and positioning with drag, zoom and focal presets. Fully offline: photos never leave your machine.

## How it works

1. Load a photo for the hero panel and one for each strip panel (or fill every panel with one photo).
2. Drag the preview to pan, scroll to zoom, or use the sliders and focal presets to frame each face.
3. Pick a preset caption (or write your own), a lettering style and a grade — Golden Hour or Neon Night.
4. Choose a post size and hit **Export PNG**. The cover downloads at full resolution, ready to share.

## Originality

Everything is drawn in code or AI-made: the skyline, sun, palms, lettering and layout are original procedural art — no game logo, artwork, characters or fonts. The typeface is Anton under the SIL Open Font License (`app/fonts/OFL.txt`). The two sample photos are AI-made and free to use.

## Run it

Serve `app/` with any static server and open `index.html` — no build step, no network requests.
